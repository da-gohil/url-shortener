About URL Shortener

Turn long, unwieldy links into short ones that are easy to share, and see how often they're used. Links are checked before they're shortened, so what you share is safe to click.

How it works

  1. 1

    Paste a long link

    Any http or https address. Leave off the https:// and it's added for you.

  2. 2

    It's checked

    We make sure the address exists and isn't somewhere a short link shouldn't point, such as a private network or a site that's been blocked.

  3. 3

    Share it

    You get a six-character link that redirects to your page. Every visit is counted.

What you can do

Without an account

  • Shorten links straight from the home page.
  • Links are public and expire after 30 days.
  • Up to 10 links an hour, to keep things fair.

With a free account

  • Private links that only you can open.
  • Choose when a link expires, or never.
  • My URLs: see clicks, search, filter and sort all your links.
  • Edit a link's visibility and expiry, or delete it.

For administrators

  • An overview of links, users and clicks across the site.
  • Disable links that shouldn't be shared, without losing their history.
  • Manage accounts and roles.
  • An audit log of every change.

Keeping links safe

Private means private
Anyone other than the owner gets the same "not found" page as a link that never existed, so private links can't be discovered by guessing.
Checked destinations
Links to private or internal networks, links hiding a username or password, and blocked sites are refused.
Expiry and moderation
Expired links stop working on their own, and an administrator can disable a harmful link at any time.
Protected accounts
Passwords are stored hashed, weak and common ones are refused, and repeated failed sign-ins lock the account for a while.
Fair use
Rate limits stop anyone flooding the site with links.
No tracking
Only a visit count is kept for each link, nothing about who clicked it.
